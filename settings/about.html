About this app

Last updated September 21, 2026
Rehabit connects you to your own physiotherapist. Your therapist prescribes exercises, you record what you actually managed, and the two of you can message each other and keep track of appointments — all in one place, instead of a paper sheet that gets lost and a phone number nobody answers.

What it does

•
Shows the exercises your physiotherapist has prescribed for you, with a video where one has been recorded.
•
Lets you record each session — sets, repetitions and a pain level from 0 to 5 — including sessions you could only partly complete.
•
Shows your history, so both of you can see how things have gone over weeks rather than relying on memory.
•
Carries a private conversation between you and your therapist.
•
Shows the appointments your therapist has booked, and lets you cancel one with a reason while it is more than 24 hours away.

What it does not do

This app is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. It does not diagnose anything, does not decide what you should do, and does not assess whether an exercise is suitable for you. Every clinical judgement in it was made by your physiotherapist — the app only records and relays it.
Nothing here replaces seeing your physiotherapist or doctor. If an exercise hurts more than your therapist told you to expect, stop and speak to them.
In an emergency, do not use this app. Call your local emergency number — 112 anywhere in the EU — or go to your nearest emergency department. Messages here are not monitored around the clock and may not be read for some time.

Who can use it

Patient accounts are created with a one-time invite code issued by your physiotherapist. Physiotherapist accounts are not self-service: the clinic clears the email address in advance, and it has to be confirmed by email before the first sign-in. That is what keeps the exercise library and every patient record behind a real professional rather than anyone who fills in a form.

Your data

What you record here is health data, and it is treated as such. Only you and the physiotherapist you are linked to can see your exercise records, your notes and your conversation. Other physiotherapists at the practice cannot. The Privacy Notice sets out exactly what is stored, where, for how long, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Language and formats

The app is available in English, Spanish and German, and dates, times and numbers follow whichever region you choose — so the same appointment can read as 15/09/2026 14:30 or September 15, 2026 at 2:30 PM depending on what you are used to. Both settings live on this device and can be changed at any time under Settings.

Accessibility

Every colour in the app has been checked against WCAG AA contrast requirements in both light and dark mode, controls meet minimum touch-target sizes, and headings, buttons and form fields are labelled for screen readers. If something is unusable for you, please tell us — that is a defect, not a preference.
